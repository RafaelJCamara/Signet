# ADR-NNN: Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by `ADR-NNN` (link it) | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed

## Context

What forced a decision. State the constraint or problem, not the answer. If a competing
product's behaviour is part of the reasoning, cite the specific behaviour rather than a
general impression.

## Decision

The choice, in the imperative: "Signet does X", not "we could do X". One paragraph.

## Alternatives considered

Each rejected option and the specific reason it lost. **This is the part a decision table
cannot hold and the part future readers actually need** — without it, someone re-proposes
a rejected option every six months.

## Consequences

What becomes true, including what gets worse. An ADR with only upside is not finished.
